The golden rule: Copilot only sees what you can see

  • Copilot can only summarize or reference content you are authorized to access.
  • Encrypted files: you need VIEW and EXTRACT rights for Copilot to use them.
  • New content created from labelled files inherits the highest-priority sensitivity label.
  • Your prompts and files are not used to train the models.

Source: Microsoft Learn — How data is protected and audited in Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Copilot; Microsoft Copilot data and compliance readiness

Before you rely on meeting recaps

  • Intelligent recap needs a Microsoft Copilot or Teams Premium licence and transcription turned on.
  • Recording gives the full recap (speakers, topics, chapters).
  • Recap without saving transcripts is in public preview: the AI summary is stored as a read-only Word document in the organizer’s OneDrive.
  • Recordings and transcripts are files too — your organization’s Purview policies apply.

At BSI

Which meetings may be recorded or transcribed is set by BSI policy. When in doubt, ask the organizer or IT — and announce transcription at the start of the meeting.

Working safely with Copilot: what’s protected, and what you do

Protected for you

  • Copilot only uses content you are already allowed to access.
  • Labelled and protected documents stay protected — and new documents inherit their label.
  • Your prompts and files are not used to train the models.

What you do

  • Check the sensitivity label before you share a Copilot draft.
  • Verify, then sign off — you own the final document.

Can’t see Copilot in your desktop app?

  1. Make sure you are signed in with your BSI work account.
  2. Open copilot.cloud.microsoft or Word / Excel / PowerPoint on the web and continue there.
  3. Close and reopen the desktop app, and install pending Office updates.
  4. Still missing? Share your work email in the chat or with the room team — we will pass it to BSI IT for a licence check.

Good to know

Copilot experiences in Word, Excel and PowerPoint can differ depending on your licence and your organization’s configuration.
